# RetailPulse – AI-Powered Customer Analytics & Demand Forecasting
Transforming Retail Data into Actionable Business Intelligence

RetailPulse is a comprehensive Power BI-based retail analytics solution designed to help businesses monitor sales performance, understand customer behavior, optimize inventory management, and forecast future demand. The project leverages interactive dashboards, KPI monitoring, and advanced data visualization techniques to support data-driven decision-making.

# Project Overview

Retail businesses generate large volumes of transactional data every day. However, extracting meaningful insights from this data can be challenging without proper analytics tools.

RetailPulse addresses this challenge by providing an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard that enables stakeholders to:

Monitor sales and profit trends
Analyze customer purchasing behavior
Forecast future demand
Track inventory performance
Compare regional sales performance
Make informed business decisions

# Business Objectives
- Improve visibility into retail operations
-Enable real-time KPI monitoring
- Optimize inventory management
- Support strategic business planning
- Identify customer purchasing patterns
- Enhance forecasting accuracy
